# story bible — «Ճշմարտության գինը» / What If Nobody Could Ever Lie To You?

**Logline.** A young person wakes up one morning with a supernatural ability: every sentence anyone speaks
is instantly marked true or false in their mind. The first day it feels like a superpower — a lying friend, a
used-phone salesman, a fake internship, a bank scam. But the ability cannot be switched off, cannot be proven to
anyone, and cannot see the truth behind an honest mistake. By the end they learn the real price of truth:
it protects people, it does not own them.

**Tone.** Mystery → cold wonder → tension → tenderness. Second person ("you"), present tense.

## Motifs
1. **Truth / lie glyph** — a small glyph near every speaker: `✓`-style gold diamond for truth, a red cracked
   diamond for a lie. It pulses on the exact word.
2. **Gold thread** — a thin glowing filament that appears whenever a lie is detected; it ties the moment to the
   viewer's eye.
3. **The three knocks** — mother's secret sign. Three golden rings on the door, timed to the words «Մեկ։ Երկու։ Երեք։»
4. **Cyan glow** — every screen (phone, LED, video wall) glows the same cyan.
5. **Cracks** — when truth breaks something, thin white cracks spread across the frame (fracture overlay).

## Key-shot options (picked one each)
1. **Hook (0:00)** — (a) slow push through the hangout while the three friends' voices overlap as floating glyphs;
   (b) top-down on a low table with three cups and three speech bubbles turning red; **(c) chosen:** hangout wide,
   three friends talk, the hero sits still and every other bubble cracks red around them.** Reason: establishes
   the power as isolation, not triumph.**
2. **Title (≈1:05)** — (a) blueprint collage of eyes; (b) a giant eye iris opening on the glyph; **(c) chosen:**
   the glyph grid collapsing into the title over the street at night, `slam` type.** Reason: matches the "one rule" beat.**
3. **Freeze frame (bank scam)** — (a) frozen rain around a phone; **(b) chosen:** frozen cash particles mid-air,
   the hero alone sharp, `1000x` badge.** Reason: the scam is the moment the power becomes public.
4. **Climax (gala)** — (a) smash cut to the LED numbers; **(b) chosen:** the LED shows the real ledger, thin white
   cracks spread over the screen, the crowd turns.** Reason: numbers beat words — the film's thesis.**
